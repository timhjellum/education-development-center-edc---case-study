The Challenge

EDC's programs and content lived across multiple platforms — separate systems, separate content models, no shared structure tying the organization together. Before any of it could move, someone had to know what existed. I led the pre-deployment work: a full content inventory, a content classification system, and a migration strategy to bring everything into one unified application.
